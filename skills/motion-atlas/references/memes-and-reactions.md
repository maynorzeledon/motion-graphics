# Meme posts, templates, and reaction assets

## Choose the right collection

- Styles and inspiration → Social post and meme styles: composition, typography, color, and editing approach. Includes meme-news cards, shitpost editing, and static social layouts.
- Meme templates: reusable blank images, editable sources, caption slots, visual joke layouts, and example finished memes.
- Reply GIFs, images and stickers: ready-made reactions matched to emotion, intent, tone, and thread context.
- Meme clips and reactions: video cutaways and comic inserts for editing.
- Comments and replies: text response voices and conversation studies.

## Use a meme template

Read the actual base image and slot manifest. Inspect the finished example if one exists. Understand the joke structure before changing text. Preserve readable text, intended panel order, safe cropping, and source records. Use `templates/meme-template.json` for collected reusable assets. Planned entries are wishlist items, not downloaded image packs.

For meme-news cards, distinguish the visual headline treatment from factual verification. The supplied screenshot is a layout reference; its headline and connection to the pictured person have not been verified. Use new factual claims only when supported, or keep parody clearly framed as parody.

## Choose a reply image, GIF, or sticker

Read the video or parent comment first. Match reaction meaning and sincerity/sarcasm to the actual conversation. Inspect the full animation and loop when available; a thumbnail may omit the relevant moment. Check legibility at small size and alpha edges for stickers. Keep original source, caption transcript, and usage notes. Use `templates/reaction-asset.json`. Do not invent availability or a successful response history.

Uploads can use the roles Meme template / blank, Reply GIF / animation, Reply image, and Transparent sticker. Device-local files are included in exported backups; committed static references are served with the website.
