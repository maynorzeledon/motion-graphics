# Creator studies

## Two separate lenses

Storytelling: premise, hook, information order, stakes, causal progression, escalation, tension, comedy logic, callbacks, and payoff.

Editing style: framing, typography, camera movement, visual effects, transitions, color, sound design, and visual rhythm.

Keep separate studies for both when a reference is useful in both ways. A creator name is an indexing aid. Study folders and their leaf names are collection prompts, not verified analyses or claims that every episode follows that structure.

## Reference packet

For each actual video, create a custom entry inside the creator’s folder, or use a reference-study slot. Attach the original video or a source link, transcript/script, timecoded visual beat sheet, storyboard frames, and analysis. Use the entry editor’s study fields and file-role selector. See `templates/story-study.json` for the portable file contract.

Record each beat as: time range | spoken line | visible action | purpose | question opened/resolved | editing technique. Identify where the video changes the viewer’s understanding, where stakes change, and how the ending pays off the opening. Capture reusable structural rules rather than copying an unrelated script verbatim.

Store files in `entries/<study-id>/` when committing material. The browser’s uploads remain device-local until exported and intentionally saved to shared storage.

## Wants and Needs — entertainment-led brand stories

Official profile checked: https://www.instagram.com/wantsandneedsbrand_/?hl=en

Collection label: **Entertainment-led ads / delayed product reveal**. The initial study prompts follow the user’s requested focus: a sketch, challenge, or curiosity hook draws attention before a clothing or brand reveal. Record the actual first product appearance, first brand mention, reveal, and callback for each supplied example. These folders contain analysis prompts, not imported videos or a completed account-wide analysis.

Use the Storytelling creator folder for narrative mechanics and the Styles creator folder for framing, transitions, staging, typography, and visual rhythm.

## Explainer coverage and program detours

Personal B-roll includes tabletop paper explanations and Vsauce-inspired physical explainer coverage: direct-to-camera presentation, hands and props, drawing, demonstrations, and solo filming sequences. These are requested shot ideas, not a verified shot analysis of a particular Vsauce video.

Storytelling has separate Emotional short films, Horror short films, Television and program storytelling, and Format-switching sketches sections. The latter includes reference collection prompts for grantbeans and jercho1: upload an actual example, timecode each format change, identify the continuing premise, and map the detour to its payoff. News, cooking, reality, sports, courtroom, game-show, and other formats can provide short narrative detours. Store their visual treatment separately under Styles → Television formats and sketch detours.
